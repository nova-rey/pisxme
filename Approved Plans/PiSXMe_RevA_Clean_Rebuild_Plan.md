# PiSXMe Rev A Clean Rebuild Plan

## Summary

Create the new design at `pisxme/reva-clean/` as `PiSXMe_RevA_Clean`. Freeze the existing `pisxme/PiSXMe.*`, legacy libraries, experiments, conflicts, and validation artifacts as `LEGACY_DONOR_REFERENCE`; never route or repair them further.

Phase 0 is currently open: NYX is on `recovery/mac-import-20260829` at `359a568`, with `bible.md` modified and 172 recovered files untracked. The recovery staging checksum passes for all 208 entries, the legacy KiCad sources retain their handoff hashes, KiCad 10.0.5 and the SKiDL→PCB fixture pass, and the recovered bridge suite is 11/12 due to one hard-coded Mac path.

Create private `nova-rey/pisxme-private` for checkpoint branches. Keep public `origin` unchanged until the external-review gate.

## Project and Interface Contract

- New project-local schematic, rules, symbols, footprints, models, validation receipts, and generated connectivity live under `pisxme/reva-clean/`.
- All new or transplanted custom assets use the project-local library namespace `PiSXMeRevAClean`, backed by `PiSXMe_RevA_Clean.kicad_sym` and `PiSXMe_RevA_Clean.pretty`.
- No legacy `PiSXMe:` symbol or footprint library identifier may appear in the clean schematic or PCB. Donor assets must be copied into the new namespace and independently revalidated before use.
- Official KiCad library identifiers may be used only when intentionally selected and included in the library/asset audit; no unresolved or machine-local global-library dependency may pass Phase 3.
- Hierarchy: `CORE_CM5`, `V100_PCIE`, `V100_POWER`, `POWER_INPUT`, `REGULATORS`, `ETHERNET`, `STORAGE`, `SERVICE`, `COOLING`, and `DEBUG`.
- CM5 exposes only PCIe Gen2 x1, native Gigabit Ethernet, one USB3 storage path, USB2 SERVICE, required control/debug, and power. No USB hub, USB Ethernet, 2.5GbE, external USB3, NVLink, PCIe switch, or unused PCIe lanes.
- Storage is CM5 USB3 → selected USB/SATA bridge → underside B-key SATA M.2. Use 2280 as the primary envelope and allow a 2242 retention point if it remains mechanically clean. Mark the board `M.2 SATA ONLY — NVMe NOT SUPPORTED`.
- SERVICE is USB2 UFP only: DP/DM, connector-boundary ESD, two 5.1 kΩ Rd resistors, and host VBUS sense/test. It never sources VBUS.
- Power contract: two mandatory 12 V inputs from a regulated, current-limited cold-plug bench supply; hot-plug is unsupported for Rev A.
- Layer contract: F.Cu signals/components, In1 solid GND, In2 low-voltage power, In3 protected 12 V/high-current power, In4 solid GND, B.Cu secondary signals/components. Ordinary through vias only; no ordinary signals on plane layers.
- SKiDL may own an island only after its hierarchy, custom pin mapping, and generated KiCad parity fixtures pass. Otherwise native hierarchical KiCad owns that island. There will be exactly one connectivity authority per block, with native KiCad reopen/ERC/netlist/PCB parity as the universal acceptance gate.

## Ownership, Commits, and Validation

- Root coordinates gates and Git. One `kicad_engineer` owns all mutable KiCad work.
- Use `pcb_architect` through the architecture/placement/stack freeze, `power_integrity_engineer` after Phases 14–15, `pcie_specialist` after Phase 16, `dfm_engineer` at Phase 24, and one final `hardware_auditor`.
- Permit one focused correction cycle per specialist gate. Do not continue after `FAIL` or unverifiable critical evidence.
- Make checkpoint commits only for validated recovery, architecture/reference closure, completed source islands, stack freeze, major routed subsystems, acreage freeze, accepted shrink iterations, and final review. Append `bible.md` in every such commit.
- Push pre-review commits only to the private mirror. Do not fabricate/order hardware or push clean-rebuild branches to public `origin` before the external-review gate.
- Preserve `REV_A_EMPIRICAL_RISK` for undocumented V100 endpoint/sequencing behavior. Design evidence cannot claim physical enumeration, thermal success, or hardware operation before fabrication and bring-up.

## Assumptions and Locked Defaults

- Git: protected recovery and `reva-clean` branches backed by new private `nova-rey/pisxme-private`.
- Libraries: custom production assets are isolated under `PiSXMeRevAClean`; legacy `PiSXMe:` IDs are forbidden in the clean project.
- Fabrication basis: refreshed current JLCPCB six-layer stack, ordinary through vias.
- Input power: regulated, current-limited cold-plug 12 V supply; both inputs mandatory.
- Storage: JMS578 preferred but not selected until Phase 2 authority closes; B-key SATA M.2, 2280 primary, optional 2242 retention.
- CM5 side and temporary acreage dimensions remain evidence-derived decisions under the deterministic Phase 10/11 gates.
- The legacy 240 × 140 mm outline, old dual-USB architecture, and historical routing are not constraints on the clean project.
