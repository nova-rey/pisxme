### Current and temperature facts

The page-6 maximum-current table is application-dependent and based on fully loaded connectors, tinned stranded copper, and a 30 °C maximum temperature rise. For 16 AWG and a 2–3 circuit connector:

| Terminal material | 16 AWG, 2–3 circuits | 16 AWG, 4–6 circuits | Relevant V2.1 implication |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Brass | 9 A/circuit | 8 A/circuit | Does not explain V2.1's retained 8 A screen for a 2-position header |
| Phosphor bronze | **8 A/circuit** | 7 A/circuit | Matches V2.1's conservative 8 A/circuit screen for each 2-position header |

Molex requires application derating for circuit size, ambient, PCB copper/trace size, adjacent heating, wire size/stranding/coating/length, and crimp quality. This table is a component/application ceiling, not proof of PiSXMe system capacity, current sharing, or thermal closure.

The temperature section lists formed brass at −40 to +80 °C operation and solid brass/phosphor bronze at −40 to +105 °C; the field-temperature statement includes a 30 °C terminal rise at maximum rated current. The specification also lists 600 VAC RMS/DC maximum, 16 AWG maximum insulation diameter 3.15 mm, 30 mating cycles, and 260 °C reflow/wave resistance requirements for the applicable header series.

## Selected 5556 alloy for V2.1

The V2.1 candidate retains an 8 A/circuit screen and declares 16 AWG minimum harness conductors. On the manufacturer table, that combination corresponds to **phosphor bronze 5556 female crimp terminals**, tin plated at mating and termination interfaces. The matching official Molex 16-AWG part records are:

- `39000080` / `39-00-0080` / `0039000080`: female crimp terminal, phosphor bronze, tin mating and termination plating, 16 AWG, bag; official alternative `39000079` reel. Molex part page: https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/39000080
- `39000079` / `39-00-0079` / `0039000079`: same phosphor-bronze/tin material system, 16 AWG-capable reel; official alternative `39000080` bag. Molex part page: https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/part-detail/39000079

Molex's current 5556 series chart also lists the brass/tin 16-AWG alternatives `39000078` bag and `39000077` reel. Those are retained as a conflicting material option, not the V2.1 selected alloy, because their 2–3 circuit table value is 9 A/circuit. Package Authority must still select the reel versus bag sales package and confirm the complete mating harness, applicator/crimp, wire length, ambient, and installation derating before Power Authority signs V2.1.

## Indexed interpretation

- **Corroborated:** 5556 female crimp + 5557 receptacle + 5569 right-angle header family; 16-AWG stranded copper applicability; 8 A/circuit for 16-AWG phosphor bronze in 2–3 circuits; required 30 °C rise basis and application derating.
- **Selected for V2.1:** phosphor bronze, tin plated, 16-AWG 5556 terminal family (`39000079` reel / `39000080` bag equivalence).
- **Still unresolved:** exact sales package, crimp tooling/process, harness length and loop resistance, ambient/adjacent heating, branch current sharing, and PCB copper temperature-rise qualification.
- **Not established:** a 6-loop system rating, source capability, or integrated board power closure. Those remain Power Authority and implementation evidence.
