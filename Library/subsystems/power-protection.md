# Power and protection brief

Power and protection are architecture-owned. Treat exact regulator, switch, TVS,
fuse, connector, return-current, thermal, and inrush limits as selected-part and
selected-fabricator questions. The project reference index identifies primary vendor
material for the selected power parts and connector/fabricator basis. For the active
MIC2545A SSD switch, use the component dossier: it supplies device facts but does not
close the required SSD rail inrush, thermal, or fault-policy decision.
