## PCB implementation facts

- All eight MDI nets are routed on `F.Cu` at 0.127 mm track width.
- No MDI through-vias are used in the official block.
- The ESDs are placed directly between the CM5 breakout and the MagJack,
  rotated -90° so the pad fields match the source ordering.
- The MagJack is the exact EDAC `A70-112-331N126` footprint at 0°.
- Center taps use `TR0_TAP` through `TR3_TAP` and a local `THD-02-R` header;
  those support routes include `B.Cu` and `In2.Cu` segments in the official
  board. These are PoE/support nets, not ordinary MDI signals.
- MagJack shield pads 19/20 are on `GND`; the ESD ground pads are also on
  `GND`. The official design uses the board's GND copper/return structure.
- LED anodes use `+3.3v` and cathodes return through `R2/R3` to CM5 LED nets.
- Native KiCad source DRC reports 67 warnings, 0 unconnected items, and 0
  footprint errors. The exact-copy disposable fixture reports 86 warnings, 0
  unconnected items, and no `tracks_crossing`, `shorting_items`, or
  `via_dangling` findings; the increase is from the fixture's detached
  library configuration. The warnings are primarily the official POE tap
  spacing rule and library-override warnings.

## Comparison against PiSXMe

The official source differs from the failed PiSXMe candidates in four material
ways: it keeps the complete MDI route on one F.Cu corridor, uses the official
TPD4EUSB30 package and pad order, places the ESDs as a compact rotated pair
immediately ahead of the MagJack, and treats center taps/support as separate
support nets rather than routing them through the MDI escape. It does not use
PHY pair remapping to solve crossings.

The disposable fixture is generated by
`phase17_cm5io_official_fixture.py` as an exact native copy of the official
PCB. An earlier attempt to delete thousands of unrelated SWIG-owned tracks
was rejected as a KiCad 10 Flatpak ABI/tooling defect; the exact-copy fixture
preserves the oracle without that transformation. This receipt does not claim
that the PiSXMe acreage route has passed.
