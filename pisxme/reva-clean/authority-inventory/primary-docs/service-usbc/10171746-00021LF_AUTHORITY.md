## Selected item

- Exact MPN: `10171746-00021LF`, Amphenol Communications Solutions (FCI).
- USB 2.0, one-port, 16 terminal contact positions, 0.50 mm pitch, right-angle
  top-mount SMT with through-hole shell stakes; 5 A total VBUS/GND rating,
  1.25 A CC rating, 10,000 mating cycles, IR reflow, 0.60 mm recommended PCB.
- Manufacturer drawing supplies the pad layout, shell geometry, pin assignment,
  and 6.5 mm connector length. The local derived pattern has 16 contact pads
  plus two shell pads and retains the source coordinate families.
