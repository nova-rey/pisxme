# Anderson PP15/45 contact-footprint evidence brief

The public manufacturer product record identifies 3-5912P1 as the 45 A right-angle bottom-row PCB contact and 3-5913P1 as the top-row contact. B02021S revision 6 supplies the 1327 housing/contact arrangement, row spacing, sample PCB layout, and accessory-hole dimensions. 1S6479 explicitly directs the assembler to B02021S for the PCB footprint. The retained B02021S sample layout does not state a contact drill/slot diameter or pad/annulus/mask treatment, and 111038S2 is the insertion/extraction-tool drawing, not a contact land-pattern drawing. The exact contact land-pattern fields remain unproven from the released records acquired here.

Conclusion: Anderson electrical/row geometry is corroborated, but an exact project-local footprint cannot be authored without guessing the contact land pattern. This is a bounded evidence gap; it does not invalidate the Molex route or establish a campaign-wide external blocker.
