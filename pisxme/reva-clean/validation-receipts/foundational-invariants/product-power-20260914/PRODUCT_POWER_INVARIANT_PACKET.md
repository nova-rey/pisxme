## Product decision and high-impact result

PiSXMe Rev A is a CM5 carrier for a V100/SXM2 accelerator with PCIe, Ethernet, USB storage, service USB, power/protection, and cooling/mechanics. The governing user product decision for this audit is:

- V100/SXM2 endpoint support shall include **300 W sustained** operation.
- Retain a **330 W design peak allowance** unless authoritative evidence requires a different or higher value.

The current selected input arrangement has two Molex `0039300020`/`39-30-0020` two-position headers, with one 12-V contact and one return contact per J5/J6 branch. The exact Molex application table for the standard 5556 two-circuit 16/18-AWG system gives **8 A per circuit** at its 30 C temperature-rise test and requires application derating; it also rejects an assumed current-sharing qualification. The two-branch table screen is therefore 16 A before derating. That is below the source current required by the product envelope before physical PCB and harness losses:

```text
P_LV = 5 V*3 A + 3.3 V*2 A + 1.1 V*1 A = 22.7 W
I_300W = (300 W + 22.7 W)/(12 V*0.90) = 29.87963 A
I_330W = (330 W + 22.7 W)/(12 V*0.90) = 32.65741 A
16 A connector-table screen < 29.87963 A continuous and 32.65741 A peak
```

This is a **foundational FAIL**, not a routing warning. The historical two-circuit/8-A-per-circuit input topology is recommended `SUPERSEDED_BY_INVARIANT INV-PWR-PRODUCT-300W` wherever it is used as a product-capacity constraint. Power Authority must redesign or requalify the input/protection/distribution architecture; the product envelope must not be lowered merely to preserve the present topology.

## Immediate work-package implications

1. `P24-POWER-ENVELOPE-REASSESS` must be replaced or versioned as an internal product/architecture correction package. The previous “external blocker” wording is too broad after the explicit 300/330 W product decision.
2. `P24-POWER-INPUT-ARCHITECTURE` owns the foundational FAILs: `INV-PWR-PRODUCT-300W`, `INV-PWR-PRODUCT-330W-PEAK`, `INV-PWR-CONTINUOUS-PATH`, `INV-PWR-PEAK-PATH`, and `INV-PWR-INPUT-TOPOLOGY`. It must obtain a Power Authority decision on a compatible connector/harness/protection topology before dependent copper routing is READY.
3. `P24-POWER-RAIL-CLOSURE` owns the independent rail/return/protection evidence gaps: `INV-PWR-BRIDGE-1V1`, `INV-PWR-BRIDGE-3V3`, `INV-PWR-CM5-5V`, `INV-PWR-PROTECTION`, `INV-PWR-SEQUENCING-SHUTDOWN`, `INV-PWR-THERMAL`, `INV-PWR-RETURN-IMPEDANCE`, and `INV-PWR-TRANSIENT-LOADSTEP`.
4. The 15 open Path-A endpoint pairs, Branch-B routing and protected 12-V corridor remain dependent on the input architecture and must not be routed against the old 8-A branch premise. Unrelated source/ERC, SI/reference, mechanics/DFM, provenance, and evidence work can remain independently queued.
5. No matrix row here claims fabricated hardware, V100 operation, load-step measurements, thermal measurements, vendor authorization, or firmware readiness.
